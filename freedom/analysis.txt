1. MAIN VISUAL DIFFERENCES
------------------------------------

OVERALL READ
- Mockup: Cinematic portfolio. Product sits inside atmosphere, light, and environment. Sections feel like scenes.

HERO
- Mockup: Art-directed lifestyle / product moment (phone in a warm interior, plant, wooden surface). Product is presented.
- Current: Large raw welcome screenshot with rounded corners on black. Product is pasted.

ATMOSPHERE
- Mockup: Photographic environments continue under/behind sections (moonlit landscape behind problem thoughts; warm blur behind the Idea phone).

PROBLEM FRAGMENTS
- Mockup: Thoughts sit as dark rounded bubbles over atmospheric photography. Object-like, cinematic.
- Current: Italic type fragments on flat black, staggered L/R, with a hairline rule. Editorial, but not art-directed.

PRODUCT PRESENTATION (IDEA / JOURNEY)
- Mockup: Phone as an object in a composed scene (device presence + environmental backdrop).
- Current: Full-bleed-ish raw UI screenshots stacked with small captions (OVERVIEW, etc.). Accurate UI, bare presentation.

SECTION 03 / PATRICIA
- Mockup: Richer focal moment. Elevated card/composition with glow and atmosphere. Reads as a designed beat.
- Current: Full original Patricia screenshot, flat on black. Correct UI, quieter presentation.

CHROME / BRAND MOMENTS
- Mockup: Stronger portfolio mark (Dot.), warmer gold accent language, hero CTA pill (View project).
- Current: Peach dot + two-line menu only. No hero CTA. More restrained brand signal.

PACING AND HIERARCHY
- Mockup: Bigger swings. Type, then a big product moment, then type again. Negative space frames scenes.
- Current: More even headline to body to screenshot rhythm. Hierarchy is clear but less dramatic.
